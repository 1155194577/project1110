1. Objective: The objective of Uno is to be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

2. Card Types: Uno is played with a deck of 108 cards, which includes four colors (red, green, blue, and yellow), numbered cards from 0 to 9 in each color, and special cards such as Skip, Reverse, Draw Two, and Wild cards.

3. Starting the Game: The game begins with each player being dealt 7 cards. The remaining cards are placed facedown to form a draw pile, and the top card from the draw pile is turned face up to start the discard pile.

4. Playing the Game: Players take turns in circle , They must match the top card of the discard pile either by number, color, or symbol. For example, if the top card is a blue 5, a player can play a blue card or any card with the number 5.

5. Special Cards:
   - Skip: When a player plays a Skip card, the next player in turn is skipped, and their turn is forfeited.
   - Reverse: When a player plays a Reverse card, the direction of play is reversed. For example, if the game was going clockwise, it will change to counterclockwise.
   - Draw Two: When a player plays a Draw Two card, the next player in turn must draw two cards from the draw pile and skip their turn.
   - Wild: Wild cards can be played on any card. When a player plays a Wild card, they must declare a new color for the game. The next player must play a card of that color or another Wild card.
